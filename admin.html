Card orders turn Paid automatically. For bank transfers, click Mark paid once the money arrives. Marking paid takes the items off stock; cancelling a paid order puts them back.

Products & Stock

Click a product to edit it. Each colour has its own photos and stock per size. To update lots of stock at once, download the sheet, edit it in Excel or Google Sheets, then upload it (.xlsx or .csv). Blank cells are left as they are.
