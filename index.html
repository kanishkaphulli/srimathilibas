Our Collections

From everyday ethnic wear to festive and bridal looks — find the outfit that feels like you.

A little piece of India, here in Auckland

Srimathi Libas was born from a simple wish — to make beautiful Indian traditional wear easy to find in New Zealand, without the guesswork of ordering from overseas.

Every piece is chosen for its fabric, finish and fit, so you can dress with confidence for weddings, festivals, poojas and every celebration in between.

  • Handpicked designsCurated pieces you won't find on every rack.
  • Local & personalAuckland-based, with friendly one-on-one help choosing your outfit.
  • For every occasionCasual, festive and bridal — all in one place.
